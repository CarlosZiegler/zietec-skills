# Text fields

- Use fields for short, specific input and communicate the requested information with a clear label and helpful hint.
- Choose the appropriate keyboard, secure-entry treatment, formatting, and return-key behavior.
- Keep focus order predictable and space related fields consistently.
- Validate at a useful moment and show actionable correction near the field without discarding entered data.
- Use native formatting for numeric input and consider clear buttons where the platform supports them.

Minimize entry on TV and Watch. Check long values, large text, localization, paste, hardware keyboards, and autofill where relevant rather than validating only a short ideal example.

Source: [Apple HIG — Text fields](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-fields). Checked 2026-09-08.
