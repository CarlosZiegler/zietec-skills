# Text views

- Use a text view for longer, multiline, styled, or editable text.
- Preserve legibility with suitable text styling and scrolling when content exceeds the available area.
- Let people select and copy useful content even when they cannot edit it.
- For editable views, choose the appropriate keyboard and maintain familiar text interactions.

Distinguish reading from editing states and verify how the view behaves with long content and larger text. Do not substitute a single-line field for a writing task that needs paragraphs and navigation within text.

Source: [Apple HIG — Text views](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/text-views). Checked 2026-09-08.
