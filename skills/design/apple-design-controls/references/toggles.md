# Toggles

- Use a toggle for a clearly named pair of opposing states, with an obvious current value.
- Choose the platform’s suitable representation instead of using switches everywhere.
- On iOS and iPadOS, use switch-style toggles in list rows; outside a list, consider a button with a selected state.
- On Mac, distinguish emphasized settings, hierarchical checkboxes, and mutually exclusive radio choices.
- Keep mixed checkbox states accurate where parent and child settings differ.

Write the setting so its enabled meaning is clear. Verify state changes and accessible values; a colored shape alone may not communicate whether the control is on or off.

Source: [Apple HIG — Toggles](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/toggles). Checked 2026-09-08.
