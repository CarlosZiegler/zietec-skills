# Disclosure controls

- Use disclosure to defer details or secondary functionality until they are relevant.
- Give a disclosure triangle a descriptive label that explains what will expand.
- Place a disclosure button near the content it reveals, and avoid multiple competing disclosure buttons within one view.
- Preserve a clear distinction between expanding content and navigating to another screen.

Read the platform-specific examples before choosing the indicator. Verify expanded and collapsed layouts, focus, and label meaning so hidden content remains discoverable without becoming a required guessing exercise.

Source: [Apple HIG — Disclosure controls](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/disclosure-controls). Checked 2026-09-08.
