# App Clips

- Complete one useful task or provide a meaningful demo immediately in a small, focused experience.
- Launch into the context that triggered the clip and avoid unnecessary accounts, setup, or web-based detours.
- Offer appropriate privacy-preserving payment and sign-in options when needed.
- Recommend the full app only at a helpful moment without obstructing completion.
- Keep notifications tightly related to the task.

Consult current invocation, size, asset, and printed-code requirements. An App Clip should provide real value independently of an installation pitch, and physical codes need their own production verification.

Source: [Apple HIG — App Clips](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-clips). Checked 2026-09-08.
