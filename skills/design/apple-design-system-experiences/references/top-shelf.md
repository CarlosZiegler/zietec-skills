# Top Shelf

- Highlight compelling, current, or personalized content with a direct path to play or learn more.
- Avoid ads and prices in this Apple TV surface.
- Use dynamic full-screen content when appropriate and provide a suitable static fallback.
- Do not draw apparent controls into a static image.
- Follow the specifications for the selected layout, artwork, and content count.

Review both focused preview behavior and the transition into the app. Top Shelf should help people reach the advertised content rather than send them to an unrelated landing screen.

Source: [Apple HIG — Top Shelf](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/top-shelf). Checked 2026-09-08.
