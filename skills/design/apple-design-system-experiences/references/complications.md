# Complications

- Choose essential, changing information suitable for repeated glances at the watch face.
- Support relevant families and consider distinct complications when people value different data.
- Deep-link each complication to the corresponding detail.
- Keep privacy and update timing deliberate, and supply useful static placeholders.
- Inspect tinted presentations and choose ring or gauge styles that match the data.

Verify current family specifications and minimum visual detail. A complication should be identifiable and useful without requiring the app to open each time the wrist is raised.

Source: [Apple HIG — Complications](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/complications). Checked 2026-09-08.
