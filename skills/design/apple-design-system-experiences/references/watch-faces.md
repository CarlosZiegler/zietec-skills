# Watch faces

- Share configurations that showcase useful combinations of your app’s complications.
- Provide accurate previews and broad device compatibility where possible.
- Handle an incompatible face gracefully.

Distinguish sharing a configured system watch face from creating an arbitrary replacement watch-face renderer. Check current supported sharing behavior and available complications before promising a design can be installed as a face.

Source: [Apple HIG — Watch faces](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/watch-faces). Checked 2026-09-08.
