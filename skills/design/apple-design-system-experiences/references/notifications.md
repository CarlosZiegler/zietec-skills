# Notifications

- Send concise, valuable information rather than repeated reminders to use the app.
- Avoid sensitive content and provide a meaningful generic preview when details are hidden.
- Offer contextual, preferably nondestructive actions that do more than merely open the app.
- Handle foreground delivery without duplicating the app’s own visible feedback.
- Keep unread badges truthful and avoid relying on them for essential information.

Design the destination and result of each action. Use an in-context error or appropriate alert for a current task failure instead of treating notifications as a general error-reporting surface.

Source: [Apple HIG — Notifications](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/notifications). Checked 2026-09-08.
