# Always On

- Retain glanceable essential information while reducing nonessential brightness and motion.
- Hide sensitive details and consider carefully which personal information remains useful and appropriate.
- Keep the layout recognizable between active and resting states.
- Transition animation naturally into its resting presentation.

Verify current device support and the specific system surface involved. Always On is a low-power, privacy-sensitive state; it is not permission to keep the full active interface continuously animating.

Source: [Apple HIG — Always On](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/always-on). Checked 2026-09-08.
