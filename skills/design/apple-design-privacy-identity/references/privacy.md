# Privacy

- Request only data needed for a clear feature, at the moment the feature requires it.
- Explain what the information enables and how it is used; avoid unnecessary permission requests at launch.
- Prefer on-device processing and established system protections where suitable.
- If an explanatory screen immediately precedes the system permission alert, provide one neutral button that leads to that alert. Avoid competing actions, imitation, or wording that pressures consent; take particular care with tracking requests.
- Keep authentication and sensitive storage within established platform mechanisms.

Design useful denied and unavailable states. Check current privacy, tracking, and distribution requirements separately; the HIG is not a complete implementation or legal checklist.

Source: [Apple HIG — Privacy](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/privacy). Checked 2026-09-08.
