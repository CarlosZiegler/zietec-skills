# Sign in with Apple

- Offer sign-in in exchange for understandable value, preserving a useful experience before authentication where possible.
- Use the system button and approved branding, size, and spacing guidance.
- Respect private relay addresses and do not ask people to supply an additional password for this method.
- Distinguish required information from optional profile details and defer optional requests.
- Make existing-account linking and the signed-in state clear when relevant.

Check current implementation and platform requirements. A successful sign-in should lead to the intended task rather than a second unexplained registration flow.

Source: [Apple HIG — Sign in with Apple](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sign-in-with-apple). Checked 2026-09-08.
