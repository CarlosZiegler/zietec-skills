# Managing accounts

- Require an account only when the core experience needs one, and delay sign-in until its benefit is clear.
- Offer appropriate platform authentication, including Sign in with Apple or passkeys where suitable.
- Identify only methods available in the current context and avoid confusing account credentials with a device passcode.
- Provide an understandable account-deletion route, timing, and consequences for purchases or subscriptions.
- Minimize entry and repeated profile selection on shared TV devices.

Separate signing out, cancelling billing, and deleting an account. People should understand which action affects access, data, and future charges.

Source: [Apple HIG — Managing accounts](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/managing-accounts). Checked 2026-09-08.
