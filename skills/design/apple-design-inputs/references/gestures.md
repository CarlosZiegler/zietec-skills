# Gestures

- Preserve familiar gesture meanings and respond promptly.
- Provide another route to important actions, particularly when a gesture is hidden, precise, or complex.
- Add custom gestures only for a concrete need and teach them in context.
- Do not conflict with system gestures; shortcut gestures should supplement familiar interactions.
- In visionOS, support comfortable direct and indirect input without requiring a particular hand or body position.

Review cancellation, simultaneous gestures, system-edge interactions, and alternate inputs. On Watch, check current double-tap guidance before assigning a primary action that competes with scrolling.

Source: [Apple HIG — Gestures](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/gestures). Checked 2026-09-08.
