# Focus and selection

- Distinguish focus, selection, and activation according to the platform and task.
- Use native focus appearance and movement where possible, with logical order for custom content.
- Avoid moving focus unexpectedly without the person’s interaction.
- On TV, leave space for focus effects and preserve separate activation when selecting would open new content.
- Use suitable focus treatment for text fields versus lists or collections.

Review every reachable target, including empty and disabled states. A visible highlight does not prove that sequential navigation reaches the correct controls or returns to a sensible place.

Source: [Apple HIG — Focus and selection](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/focus-and-selection). Checked 2026-09-08.
