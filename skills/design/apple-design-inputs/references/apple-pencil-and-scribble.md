# Apple Pencil and Scribble

- Make drawing begin promptly at contact, with feedback that tracks the mark and supported pressure or angle behavior.
- Accommodate both hands and transitions between Pencil and finger input.
- Use hover to preview an effect, not to commit an action.
- Respect double-tap preferences; keep squeeze actions discrete and reversible, and reserve barrel roll for marking behavior.
- Support Scribble in relevant fields, keeping the writing area stable and spacious while people write.

Verify hardware capabilities before promising hover, squeeze, or roll. Test latency, palm interaction, and handedness on compatible devices when available.

Source: [Apple HIG — Apple Pencil and Scribble](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/apple-pencil-and-scribble). Checked 2026-09-08.
