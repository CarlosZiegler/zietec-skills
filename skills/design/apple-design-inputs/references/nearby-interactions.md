# Nearby interactions

- Design from a meaningful physical-world task, using distance, direction, and context.
- Give continuous, understandable feedback as relative position changes.
- Provide an alternative way to complete the task.
- Explain positioning and intervening obstacles when they affect the experience.
- Use the platform’s permission and privacy-preserving session model.

Verify compatible hardware and test in real environments. Proximity alone does not establish intent, successful transfer, or permission to perform an unrelated action.

Source: [Apple HIG — Nearby interactions](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/nearby-interactions). Checked 2026-09-08.
