# Pointing devices

- Preserve familiar mouse and trackpad gestures and system-wide meanings.
- Use standard pointer shapes and effects for standard interactions.
- Provide comfortable hit regions, including adjacent toolbar targets, even when a pointer offers precision.
- Keep touch, pointer, and keyboard routes consistent in purpose.
- Use custom hover or pointer effects only to communicate useful state or behavior.

Verify selection, modifiers, hidden controls, and switching between input devices. Hover should help predict an action without becoming the sole way to discover essential information.

Source: [Apple HIG — Pointing devices](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/pointing-devices). Checked 2026-09-08.
