# Camera Control

- Use concise control names, recognizable symbols, and units for displayed values.
- Keep the viewfinder clear enough for the system overlay and minimize competing controls during adjustment.
- Offer controls that fit the active camera mode, with meaningful default or prominent values.
- Support direct launch into the camera experience where available.

Verify current device compatibility and APIs; the source’s original examples are not an exhaustive list of future hardware. Review the bezel interaction on a real supported device before asserting ergonomic quality.

Source: [Apple HIG — Camera Control](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/camera-control). Checked 2026-09-08.
