# Digital Crown

- On Watch, use the Crown for expected navigation and appropriate data inspection.
- Provide visible feedback that follows rotation speed, with native haptics when they fit the task.
- Keep the active effect of rotation understandable.
- On Vision Pro, preserve the Crown’s system role rather than treating it as an arbitrary app control.

Check platform-specific guidance before assigning behavior. Crown input should complement the visible task and remain predictable when the view changes.

Source: [Apple HIG — Digital Crown](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/digital-crown). Checked 2026-09-08.
