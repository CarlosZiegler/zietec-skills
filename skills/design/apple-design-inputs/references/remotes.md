# Remotes

- Preserve standard remote gestures, focus behavior, Back navigation, and playback controls.
- Distinguish a deliberate press from a light or accidental touch.
- Provide immediate feedback for navigation and content changes.
- Make custom gestures rare and understandable.
- For supported live-TV remotes, handle guide browsing and channel navigation according to expected button meanings.

Test sequential navigation and repeated Back presses from realistic depths. A television interface should remain usable without a pointer or an unexplained collection of gesture shortcuts.

Source: [Apple HIG — Remotes](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/remotes). Checked 2026-09-08.
