# Gyroscope and accelerometer

- Use motion sensing only when it gives people a clear benefit.
- Outside active gameplay, avoid making device motion the primary method of manipulating the interface.
- Verify sensor and platform support before depending on a measurement.

Provide a usable alternative when motion cannot be performed comfortably or reliably. Consider how people use the device while seated, mounted, or moving in the real world, rather than assuming a single ideal posture.

Source: [Apple HIG — Gyroscope and accelerometer](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/gyro-and-accelerometer). Checked 2026-09-08.
