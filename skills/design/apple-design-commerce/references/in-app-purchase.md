# In-app purchase

- Describe digital products and subscription benefits clearly, with the total billing price and relevant recurring terms.
- Let people experience value before prompting for purchase and use the standard confirmation experience.
- Keep options distinguishable and avoid asking an existing subscriber to subscribe again.
- Make restoration, subscription management, cancellation, and purchase help discoverable where applicable.
- Explain Family Sharing accurately when supported and avoid inventing refund-policy promises.

Verify current App Store rules for the actual region and distribution model. Design success, pending, cancelled, failed, and already-owned states without treating every dismissal as a payment error.

Source: [Apple HIG — In-app purchase](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/in-app-purchase). Checked 2026-09-08.
