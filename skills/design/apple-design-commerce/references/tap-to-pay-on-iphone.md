# Tap to Pay on iPhone

- Prepare merchant setup and administrative acceptance before the customer is waiting.
- Provide clear education about supported payment methods and physical presentation.
- Make Tap to Pay discoverable alongside supported alternatives, with the final amount established before acceptance begins.
- Show authorization progress and an unmistakable success or decline result.
- Offer recovery, alternate acceptance, and helpful error guidance when a transaction cannot complete.

Distinguish merchant payment acceptance from a shopper’s Apple Pay checkout. Verify supported devices, provider integration, and regional availability before promising the flow can run.

Source: [Apple HIG — Tap to Pay on iPhone](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/tap-to-pay-on-iphone). Checked 2026-09-08.
