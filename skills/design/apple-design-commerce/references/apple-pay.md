# Apple Pay

- Use Apple Pay for an eligible transaction and the system payment sheet for a coherent checkout.
- Gather essential product choices before opening payment, and use payment-sheet information rather than asking for it again.
- Avoid forcing account creation before a purchase when it is unnecessary.
- Make costs, shipping, and relevant coupon handling clear; report transaction results in the payment flow and provide confirmation afterward.
- Use approved buttons and marks for their intended roles.

Verify current product, regional, browser, and platform requirements. Do not conflate a payment acceptance logo with a button that starts payment or treat a successful-looking screen as evidence of a completed charge.

Source: [Apple HIG — Apple Pay](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/apple-pay). Checked 2026-09-08.
