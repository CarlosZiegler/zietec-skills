# Designing for tvOS

- Design for reading and choosing content from across a room, using clear hierarchy and generous, legible artwork and text.
- Use the system focus model. People should always understand which item their remote will activate, and layouts need room for focus effects.
- Preserve familiar remote navigation and playback gestures. Focus movement and activation should remain distinct when activation changes context.
- Emphasize immersive media and sound while keeping navigation understandable.
- Support shared-device use with infrequent authentication and appropriate profile changes when the active viewer changes.

Review the experience with a remote at realistic viewing distance. A layout that is easy to click on a computer may still be difficult to navigate sequentially or read on a television.

Source: [Apple HIG — Designing for tvOS](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-tvos). Checked 2026-09-08.
