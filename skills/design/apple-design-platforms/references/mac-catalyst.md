# Mac Catalyst

- Begin with an iPad app that already handles keyboard input, drag and drop, multitasking, and multiple windows well.
- Choose the intended Catalyst idiom deliberately. Audit typography, image sizing, layout assumptions, and custom appearance after that choice.
- Preserve access to destinations that were previously exposed by an iPad tab bar; provide familiar Mac routes for navigation.
- Move appropriate commands into the toolbar and menu system. Reconsider bottom-edge or side controls in favor of a natural desktop working order.
- Adapt pointer and keyboard interactions and provide a platform-appropriate app icon.

Check the live source and SDK for idiom-specific behavior. A successful build does not verify that the port has suitable control density, command organization, or window behavior.

Source: [Apple HIG — Mac Catalyst](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/mac-catalyst). Checked 2026-09-08.
