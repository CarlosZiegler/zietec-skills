# Designing for watchOS

- Deliver essential information and a focused action within a short glance or a few interactions.
- Keep hierarchy shallow. Use the Digital Crown for familiar vertical navigation or appropriate inspection of values.
- Treat complications and notifications as substantial parts of the product experience; they may be used more often than the app itself.
- Make the app independently useful while allowing system surfaces to lead directly to relevant detail.
- Show timely, contextually relevant information, and keep text and controls usable while people are moving.
- Plan for Always On behavior with readable essential content, reduced activity, and appropriate protection of personal information.

Avoid translating a phone’s full workflow to the wrist. Choose a smaller task and make it complete, with meaningful status and a clear result.

Source: [Apple HIG — Designing for watchOS](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-watchos). Checked 2026-09-08.
