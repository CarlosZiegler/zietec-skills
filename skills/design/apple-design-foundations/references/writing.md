# Writing

- Establish a consistent product vocabulary and voice, then adapt tone to the situation: celebration, instruction, and failure need different treatment.
- Use concise, action-oriented labels that predict the result. Prefer meaningful link text over generic directions.
- Keep language accessible and localizable. Avoid unexplained jargon, culture-dependent jokes, and assumptions about identity.
- Make empty states useful with an appropriate next action. Place errors near the problem and explain how to recover without blaming the person.
- Keep multi-step labels, capitalization, and terminology consistent; use the correct interaction words for the device.

Review text in its real layout, including long translations and assistive output. Text should remove uncertainty about the task, not merely make a screen sound friendly.

Source: [Apple HIG — Writing](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/writing). Checked 2026-09-08.
