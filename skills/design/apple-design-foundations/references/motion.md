# Motion

- Give motion a communication purpose: continuity, feedback, status, or an understandable change of state.
- Keep feedback brief and responsive to the person’s action. Repeated routine interactions seldom need extra animation.
- Allow interruption or cancellation when appropriate, and honor preferences that reduce motion.
- Prefer established component motion when it already describes the interaction correctly.
- In spatial experiences, avoid sustained oscillation, unnecessary peripheral movement, and moving large surroundings in ways that can disorient people. Consider fades for relocation and a stable reference where useful.

Evaluate the transition with real input and reduced-motion settings. A polished animation preview is insufficient if the transition delays control, loses context, or becomes uncomfortable in repeated use.

Source: [Apple HIG — Motion](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion). Checked 2026-09-08.
