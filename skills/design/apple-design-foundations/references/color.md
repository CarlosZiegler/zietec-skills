# Color

- Define colors by meaning and keep those meanings consistent. Prefer dynamic semantic system colors for standard roles.
- Do not copy a system color’s current RGB value into a fixed token or reuse a semantic role for unrelated meaning.
- Test light, dark, increased-contrast, translucent, and content-dependent contexts on representative displays and in different lighting.
- Pair important color distinctions with labels, shapes, or other cues. Consider cultural interpretation as well as visual accessibility.
- Apply tint carefully to Liquid Glass and its labels; colorful content underneath can reduce clarity.
- Preserve appropriate image color profiles and inspect wide-color assets where supported.

Choose brand color variants by their behavior in each context, not by assuming one accent works equally well on every background.

Source: [Apple HIG — Color](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color). Checked 2026-09-08.
