# Typography

- Use text styles and weight to express information hierarchy, maintaining that hierarchy as text grows.
- Prefer system fonts and styles when they fit the product. Custom fonts need equivalent scaling, legibility, and accessibility behavior.
- Avoid fragile thin weights at small sizes. Evaluate type at realistic device viewing distances.
- Reflow layouts at larger sizes; keep essential text available instead of truncating it or shrinking it to fit fixed containers.
- Scale meaningful symbols with associated text. Keep the number of typefaces limited enough that hierarchy remains clear.

Verify current per-platform recommended sizes, Dynamic Type tables, and tracking guidance when exact values are needed. Do not treat one iPhone body size as the minimum for every platform or every text role.

Source: [Apple HIG — Typography](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/typography). Checked 2026-09-08.
