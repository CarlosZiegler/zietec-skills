# Designing for visionOS

- Choose windows, volumes, and immersion based on the experience rather than using 3D everywhere.
- Use windows for contained, familiar tasks and reserve distinctive spatial moments for content that benefits.
- Support comfortable eye-and-hand interaction, alternate inputs, and accessibility needs.
- Use spatial audio and shared activities when they deepen the task meaningfully.
- Prioritize physical and visual comfort and continued awareness of the surroundings.

Validate the spatial relationship between content, controls, and the wearer. A flat mockup cannot establish viewing distance, targeting comfort, or safe transitions between immersion levels.

Source: [Apple HIG — Designing for visionOS](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-visionos). Checked 2026-09-08.
