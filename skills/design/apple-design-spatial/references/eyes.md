# Eyes

- Treat looking as targeting, with appropriate system hover feedback and a separate intentional activation when required.
- Use standard components and provide alternate interaction methods.
- Keep targets well spaced, comfortably placed, and visually distinguishable.
- Avoid distracting full-field patterns or abrupt custom hover changes.
- For a composite target, provide a coherent shape the system can highlight.

Test any custom hover delay and expanded state. Do not infer consent to an action merely from gaze or design around an assumption that the app receives unrestricted eye-tracking information.

Source: [Apple HIG — Eyes](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/eyes). Checked 2026-09-08.
