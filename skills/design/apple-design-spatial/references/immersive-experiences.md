# Immersive experiences

- Distinguish Shared Space from Full Space and choose mixed, progressive, or full immersion intentionally.
- Prefer an approachable initial experience and reserve deeper immersion for meaningful content.
- Keep key moments accessible at more than one immersion level where possible.
- Preserve comfort through restrained motion, appropriate passthrough, and a grounded environment.
- Avoid encouraging physical movement while the real environment is obscured.

Design entry, exit, interruption, and recentering as part of the experience. Do not require immersion merely to reach ordinary controls or assume that a full-surrounding image creates a convincing spatial environment.

Source: [Apple HIG — Immersive experiences](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/immersive-experiences). Checked 2026-09-08.
