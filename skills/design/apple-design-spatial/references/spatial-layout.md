# Spatial layout

- Center important content within comfortable view and avoid attaching ordinary content rigidly to head movement.
- Use depth cues and hierarchy accurately; do not add depth to text without a clear purpose.
- Choose fixed or dynamic scale based on whether the object represents a real physical size.
- Leave sufficient spacing for eye targeting and minimize required body movement.
- Avoid excessive windows and preserve system recentering behavior.

Review spatial layout from the wearer’s position, including near and far content. Useful depth should clarify relationships rather than produce decorative layers that strain focus.

Source: [Apple HIG — Spatial layout](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/spatial-layout). Checked 2026-09-08.
