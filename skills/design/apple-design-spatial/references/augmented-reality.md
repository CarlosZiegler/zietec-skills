# Augmented reality

- Offer AR only on capable devices and provide clear coaching for tracking and surface discovery.
- Integrate placed objects promptly, using convincing scale, lighting, and environmental relationships.
- Prefer familiar direct manipulation with sufficient interaction tolerance.
- Keep instructions and controls understandable without filling the physical scene with text.
- Account for varying environments, comfort, and physical safety, introducing movement gradually.

Design placement failure, lost tracking, offscreen objects, and recovery. Use official AR badges as specified. Validate real-world behavior rather than treating one successful placement as proof of a robust experience.

Source: [Apple HIG — Augmented reality](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/augmented-reality). Checked 2026-09-08.
