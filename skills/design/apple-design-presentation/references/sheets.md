# Sheets

- Use a sheet for a short task closely tied to the current context.
- Make completion, cancellation, and any internal Back behavior distinct.
- Avoid stacking sheets or using them for prolonged independent workflows.
- On iPhone, consider appropriate detents, a grabber for resizing, and swipe dismissal.
- On Mac, keep other windows usable and prefer a panel for repeated adjustments with live results.

Account for unsaved changes and the target platform’s presentation style. The visionOS sheet relationship differs from a phone sheet emerging from the bottom; do not universalize one animation.

Source: [Apple HIG — Sheets](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sheets). Checked 2026-09-08.
