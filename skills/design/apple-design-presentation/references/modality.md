# Modality

- Use a modal presentation only when isolating attention has a clear benefit.
- Keep the task bounded and identifiable, with an obvious way to finish or leave.
- Avoid a nested application experience inside repeated modal layers.
- Confirm dismissal only when needed to avoid meaningful data loss; otherwise preserve a simple exit.
- Close an existing modal before presenting another where possible.

For complex, sustained work, consider ordinary navigation or a dedicated full-screen experience. Specify what is saved, cancelled, or retained on dismissal instead of leaving the meaning of Close ambiguous.

Source: [Apple HIG — Modality](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/modality). Checked 2026-09-08.
