# Popovers

- Use a popover for a small amount of supplementary information or functionality anchored to relevant content.
- Keep one visible at a time and avoid presenting another layer over it.
- Preserve work when a nonmodal popover closes automatically.
- Keep size and placement appropriate, and transition resizing smoothly.
- Avoid compact-view popovers on iOS and iPadOS when another presentation is more suitable.

A warning is not a good popover task. Use a close or confirmation control only when it contributes needed clarity, and name the task rather than the component in user-facing help.

Source: [Apple HIG — Popovers](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/popovers). Checked 2026-09-08.
