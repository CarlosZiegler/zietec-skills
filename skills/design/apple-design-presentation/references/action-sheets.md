# Action sheets

- Present choices related to an action the person deliberately initiated.
- Keep titles, optional explanations, and the action list short enough to avoid unnecessary scrolling.
- Clearly distinguish destructive choices and provide cancellation when appropriate to prevent unwanted loss.
- Use the platform’s native confirmation-dialog or action-sheet presentation.

On Apple Watch, keep the total button count especially small; the source recommends no more than four including Cancel. Do not use an action sheet to deliver an unrelated urgent warning, which calls for different presentation semantics.

Source: [Apple HIG — Action sheets](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/action-sheets). Checked 2026-09-08.
