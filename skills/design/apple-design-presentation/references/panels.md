# Panels

- Use a Mac panel for supplementary controls or inspection related to the active document or selection.
- Favor simple adjustments and a concise descriptive title.
- Show, hide, and update the panel according to the working context.
- Keep panel behavior distinct from document windows, including menu-list and minimization behavior.
- Prefer standard panel styling; use a small HUD treatment only where it suits the experience.

Choose a panel when people need to adjust values repeatedly while observing the main content. A modal sheet would unnecessarily block that work.

Source: [Apple HIG — Panels](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/panels). Checked 2026-09-08.
