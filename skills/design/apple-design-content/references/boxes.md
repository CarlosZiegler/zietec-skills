# Boxes

- Use a box to identify a relatively small, logically related group of information or controls.
- Provide a brief descriptive title only when it clarifies the group.
- Use spacing and alignment to express relationships inside the box instead of repeatedly nesting borders.

Choose grouping according to meaning. A screen of unrelated bordered cards can weaken hierarchy, while a restrained group can make a form or inspector easier to scan.

Source: [Apple HIG — Boxes](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/boxes). Checked 2026-09-08.
