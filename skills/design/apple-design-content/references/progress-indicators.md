# Progress indicators

- Use determinate progress when the amount of work is known and report advancement honestly.
- Use an indeterminate state when needed, moving to determinate behavior when progress becomes measurable.
- Keep placement stable and avoid switching between circular and bar forms during the task.
- Offer cancellation when feasible, explaining meaningful consequences before stopping.
- Remove the indicator when work completes and provide context only when it is useful.

Inspect stalls, retries, and background work. Do not simulate a precise percentage without evidence, or block the entire interface for work that can proceed independently.

Source: [Apple HIG — Progress indicators](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/progress-indicators). Checked 2026-09-08.
