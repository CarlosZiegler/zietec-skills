# Rating indicators

- Use a rating indicator for a ranking or score, with symbols whose meaning people recognize.
- Make adjustment easy when the rating is editable.
- Keep spacing consistent and verify how the native component rounds values; this indicator displays whole symbols rather than partial ones.

Distinguish viewing an existing rating from entering one. A custom symbol should remain understandable and accessible, not merely match the brand.

Source: [Apple HIG — Rating indicators](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/rating-indicators). Checked 2026-09-08.
