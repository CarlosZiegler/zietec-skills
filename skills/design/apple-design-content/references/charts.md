# Charts

- Select marks, axes, and ranges to communicate the actual question in the data.
- Use meaningful bounds and familiar tick sequences; avoid scales that misrepresent comparisons.
- Provide a descriptive title or summary and maintain a clear hierarchy among data, labels, and grid lines.
- Do not hide essential information behind interaction or rely only on color.
- Make interactive data accessible through appropriate semantics and navigation, considering Audio Graphs where useful.

Verify units, formatting, legends, and keyboard or assistive interaction. Describe what the data means rather than only describing the graphic’s appearance.

Source: [Apple HIG — Charts](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/charts). Checked 2026-09-08.
