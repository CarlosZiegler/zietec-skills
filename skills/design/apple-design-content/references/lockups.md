# Lockups

- Use a lockup to combine related content, a header, and a footer into one focusable unit on supported platforms.
- Keep sizes consistent within a row or group and leave enough space for focused expansion.
- Choose the appropriate card, caption, monogram, or poster treatment for the content.
- Prefer a recognizable image over initials for a person when available.

Review all focus states and nearby elements together. A lockup should behave as one coherent target rather than a set of disconnected labels and images.

Source: [Apple HIG — Lockups](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/lockups). Checked 2026-09-08.
