# VoiceOver

- Give meaningful controls and images concise, context-specific descriptions. Do not redundantly repeat nearby text or describe purely decorative imagery.
- Keep accessible names and values synchronized with the visible state.
- Expose the meaning and interaction of charts and custom graphics, including accessible routes to important data.
- Group content into understandable units and provide an order that reflects how people need to navigate the task.
- For custom controls, supply the appropriate accessibility behavior rather than only a label.

Navigate the actual flow with VoiceOver: discover an action, activate it, understand its result, and recover from errors. A list of labels does not verify whether focus changes or interactive content are understandable in context.

Source: [Apple HIG — VoiceOver](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/voiceover). Checked 2026-09-08.
