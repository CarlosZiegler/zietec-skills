# Inclusion

- Examine assumptions in language, examples, images, defaults, and required personal information.
- Use direct, respectful language that people with different education, cultural backgrounds, and abilities can understand.
- Avoid stereotypes, exclusionary expressions, unnecessary gender assumptions, and humor that depends on shared cultural context.
- Represent people and circumstances with appropriate variety. Respect how specific individuals and communities describe themselves.
- Treat accessibility and localization as part of inclusion from the outset, not as a later cosmetic pass.

Review the experience from perspectives beyond the design team’s own. Prioritize concrete barriers such as an unsuitable required field, an inaccessible instruction, or a misleading default over a superficial checklist of diverse imagery.

Source: [Apple HIG — Inclusion](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/inclusion). Checked 2026-09-08.
