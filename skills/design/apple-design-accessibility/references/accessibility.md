# Accessibility

- Make essential information perceivable through more than one channel; color, sound, motion, or haptics alone are insufficient.
- Support larger text and layout reflow, meaningful contrast, and system accessibility preferences. Verify platform-specific target sizes and text guidance in the source.
- Keep important actions operable with alternate input methods and without precise, complex, or time-sensitive gestures.
- Provide captions or equivalent access to important audio, and describe meaningful visual information.
- Reduce cognitive burden through familiar behavior and clear recovery. Respect Reduce Motion, flashing-light accommodations, and relevant simplified experiences.

Test complete tasks with accessibility features enabled. Automated checks and visual inspection help find defects, but do not establish that custom controls, focus, media, and error recovery are usable.

Source: [Apple HIG — Accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility). Checked 2026-09-08.
