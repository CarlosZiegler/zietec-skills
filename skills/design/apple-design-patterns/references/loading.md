# Loading

- Present useful content as early as possible and keep unrelated work available while loading proceeds.
- Move large downloads into the background when feasible.
- Explain unavoidable waits with appropriate progress and meaningful context.
- For games, a tailored loading view may support the experience, but it should not hide whether work is advancing.
- On Watch, minimize the need to show a loading indicator at all.

Specify partial content, failed loading, retry, and cancellation where applicable. The best loading design often comes from changing when work occurs rather than decorating a blocking screen.

Source: [Apple HIG — Loading](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/loading). Checked 2026-09-08.
