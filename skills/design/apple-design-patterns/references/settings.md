# Settings

- Choose good defaults and offer settings only when customization serves a real need.
- Keep task-specific options close to the task and infrequently changed general settings in the expected settings area.
- Respect system preferences instead of duplicating them unnecessarily.
- Avoid using settings as a substitute for obtaining information appropriately during the task.
- On Mac, follow the established settings menu and window conventions.

Review whether a proposed setting resolves a valid difference in needs or merely transfers an unfinished design decision to the person.

Source: [Apple HIG — Settings](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/settings). Checked 2026-09-08.
