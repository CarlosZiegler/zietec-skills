# Drag and drop

- Support drag and drop where it naturally moves or copies content, with a discoverable alternative.
- Make accepted destinations and expected results visible during the drag.
- Choose appropriate representations, preferring the richest format the destination can use.
- Preserve selection and provide feedback for transfers, rejected drops, or failures; support undo when feasible.
- Respect platform copy-versus-move conventions, including copying across apps.

Test multi-item operations, autoscroll, cancelled drops, and content from other applications. Dragging is a data operation with recovery semantics, not merely an animation.

Source: [Apple HIG — Drag and drop](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/drag-and-drop). Checked 2026-09-08.
