# Collaboration and sharing

- Make the Share entry point easy to find and use system sharing or collaboration surfaces.
- Offer a small, understandable set of access and permission options.
- Distinguish sending a copy from collaborating on shared content.
- Once collaboration begins, expose the relevant collaboration controls and participant context.
- Add custom actions or event notifications only when they improve the shared task.

Specify who can access or edit the item and how people manage that access later. Review the transition from private content to collaboration instead of assuming that choosing a destination establishes the desired permissions.

Source: [Apple HIG — Collaboration and sharing](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/collaboration-and-sharing). Checked 2026-09-08.
