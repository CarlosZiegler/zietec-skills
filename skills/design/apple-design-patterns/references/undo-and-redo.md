# Undo and redo

- Make reversal available for meaningful actions, including multiple steps when appropriate.
- Describe what will be undone or redone and make the result visible.
- Preserve standard gestures, menus, and shortcuts.
- Consider reverting a meaningful group of changes when that matches the person’s task.
- Add dedicated buttons only where their visibility improves access.

Review the granularity of each undo step and the state after repeated reversal. A forgiving design needs predictable outcomes, not just a generic Undo command whose target is unclear.

Source: [Apple HIG — Undo and redo](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/undo-and-redo). Checked 2026-09-08.
