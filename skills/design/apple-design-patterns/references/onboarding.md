# Onboarding

- Let people learn through useful interaction, with optional contextual tips where possible.
- Keep any prerequisite introduction brief and focused on the app’s particular experience.
- Defer nonessential setup, customization, purchase requests, and ratings until people have experienced value.
- Avoid making large downloads or licensing text dominate the first-use flow.
- Explain a necessary permission at the point where the core experience depends on it.

Separate onboarding from launch. Design a clear path into the product and a way to skip optional education; do not require people to memorize a tour before doing a task.

Source: [Apple HIG — Onboarding](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/onboarding). Checked 2026-09-08.
