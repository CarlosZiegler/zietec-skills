# Launching

- Reach usable content quickly and restore the previous working state when appropriate.
- If a launch screen is required, make it resemble the initial interface to create a smooth transition.
- Keep launch screens free of promotional content and text that cannot adapt appropriately.
- Place an optional branded introduction in onboarding rather than delaying every launch.

Review cold launch, relaunch, and returning from interruption separately. In spatial experiences, consider starting in Shared Space; in live media, consider whether immediate playback fits the expected task.

Source: [Apple HIG — Launching](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/launching). Checked 2026-09-08.
