# Ratings and reviews

- Request a rating after meaningful engagement, at a pause that does not interrupt work or play.
- Prefer the system prompt and avoid repeated pressure.
- Let people continue using the app regardless of whether they respond.
- Consider the tradeoff before resetting a store summary rating.

Treat the prompt as an occasional request after value, not part of launch or mandatory onboarding. Check current system behavior and policies before assuming the prompt will appear on every request.

Source: [Apple HIG — Ratings and reviews](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/ratings-and-reviews). Checked 2026-09-08.
