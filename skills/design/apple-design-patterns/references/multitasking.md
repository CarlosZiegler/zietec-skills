# Multitasking

- Treat switching apps as normal, preserving enough context for a smooth return.
- Pause work that depends on the person’s active attention while allowing suitable user-initiated operations to finish in the background.
- Handle audio interruptions deliberately and use notifications sparingly.
- Adapt to supported multitasking and window configurations without interfering with system controls.
- In visionOS, do not equate looking away from video with an instruction to pause it.

Review foreground, background, inactive, and restored states. Define which operations continue and which pause based on the task rather than applying a blanket rule.

Source: [Apple HIG — Multitasking](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/multitasking). Checked 2026-09-08.
