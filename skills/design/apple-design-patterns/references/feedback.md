# Feedback

- Communicate current state, available actions, and results where people are already working.
- Use more than one channel for essential feedback so color, sound, or haptics are not the only explanation.
- Reserve interruptive alerts for critical, actionable information.
- Warn about unexpected irreversible loss, and confirm significant completion when useful.
- Explain unavailable commands in a way that helps the person proceed.

Match the strength and location of feedback to consequence and urgency. Routine success rarely needs a modal interruption, while a silent failure can leave people uncertain whether their action happened.

Source: [Apple HIG — Feedback](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/feedback). Checked 2026-09-08.
