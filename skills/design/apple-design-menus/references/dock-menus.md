# Dock menus

- Use custom Dock menu items for high-value actions or content people may want without first activating the app.
- Preserve system-provided items and make custom actions accessible elsewhere too.
- Keep labels immediately understandable in the limited context of the Dock.

This is a Mac-specific entry point. iPhone and iPad use Home Screen quick actions for a related experience, with their own guidance. Do not build a second, inconsistent command vocabulary for an action that already exists in the app.

Source: [Apple HIG — Dock menus](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/dock-menus). Checked 2026-09-08.
