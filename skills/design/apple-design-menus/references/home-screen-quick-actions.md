# Home Screen quick actions

- Offer a small set of valuable tasks that benefit from launching directly into the right context.
- Use succinct labels and familiar symbols rather than emoji in place of interface icons.
- Dynamic actions may reflect recent content, but changes should remain predictable.
- Take the person to the intended task or content immediately after activation.

Choose actions by repeated usefulness, not by how many features the app can expose. Review what happens when personalized content is unavailable, deleted, or requires authentication.

Source: [Apple HIG — Home Screen quick actions](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/home-screen-quick-actions). Checked 2026-09-08.
