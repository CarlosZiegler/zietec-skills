# Playing video

- Prefer the system player and expected playback interactions.
- Preserve the video’s original aspect ratio and use appropriate presentation modes.
- Start expected content promptly, restore suitable playback position, and avoid redundant confirmation before resuming.
- Prevent conflicting audio as presentation changes.
- Return to relevant content when playback ends or exits, including immediate exits.

Consider Picture in Picture, accessibility, external controls, and platform-specific video presentation. Check real playback and transitions instead of relying only on a poster image and transport controls.

Source: [Apple HIG — Playing video](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-video). Checked 2026-09-08.
