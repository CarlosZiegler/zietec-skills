# ShazamKit

- Use audio recognition for a clear task such as identification or a synchronized experience.
- End recording as soon as enough audio has been captured.
- Let people opt into adding recognized songs to their iCloud library.

Make listening, recognition, and completion states understandable. Distinguish recognizing a sound from permission to retain it or modify a person’s library.

Source: [Apple HIG — ShazamKit](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/shazamkit). Checked 2026-09-08.
