# Live Photos

- Keep the motion, audio, and still-photo parts intact and apply adjustments consistently across frames.
- Distinguish Live Photos from still images using the established badge treatment.
- Show downloading and playable states clearly.
- Preserve rich content when sharing to compatible destinations and provide a suitable still fallback elsewhere.

Test the complete media object rather than only its preview frame. A successful still-image export does not prove that motion and sound remain available.

Source: [Apple HIG — Live Photos](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-photos). Checked 2026-09-08.
