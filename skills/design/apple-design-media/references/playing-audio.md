# Playing audio

- Choose audio-session behavior that fits the app’s role and respects silence, volume, routing, and other playback.
- Use system volume controls; do not change the person’s overall volume unexpectedly.
- Decide how interruptions, headphone changes, backgrounding, and resumption affect playback.
- Support external controls when the app is the appropriate playback target.
- Use custom controls only for needs the system player cannot serve.

For spatial experiences, place and vary sound deliberately. Test real route and interruption transitions; a play button’s appearance cannot verify correct audio behavior.

Source: [Apple HIG — Playing audio](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-audio). Checked 2026-09-08.
