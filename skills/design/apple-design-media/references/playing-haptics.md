# Playing haptics

- Preserve the documented meaning of system haptic patterns.
- Pair haptics with other feedback and use them consistently for the same events.
- Prefer brief, purposeful effects for discrete actions; avoid unnecessary repetition.
- Make custom haptics optional and account for devices without equivalent support.

Evaluate the physical sensation on supported hardware and how it combines with sound and visuals. Haptics should reinforce a meaningful event, not become the only way to discover a result or an obstacle.

Source: [Apple HIG — Playing haptics](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-haptics). Checked 2026-09-08.
