# AirPlay

- Prefer system playback and routing UI, supporting expected streaming, mirroring, and remote control behavior.
- Send only the content people expect to the external display or speakers.
- Allow appropriate playback to continue while the device locks or the app backgrounds.
- Avoid interrupting another app merely because your app opens.
- Keep other app tasks available during playback where possible.

Use approved AirPlay naming and assets according to the source. Do not create a custom branded routing button without checking the current restrictions and system alternatives.

Source: [Apple HIG — AirPlay](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/airplay). Checked 2026-09-08.
