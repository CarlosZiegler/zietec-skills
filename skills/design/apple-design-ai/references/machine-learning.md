# Machine learning

- Decide whether predictions are essential or supplementary, proactive or requested, visible or implicit, and private or shared.
- Make explicit feedback optional, understandable, and consequential; reflect corrections promptly and persist them.
- Use multiple signals thoughtfully and avoid trapping people in a narrowing feedback loop.
- Protect sensitive suggestions and let people control relevant information.
- Calibrate expectations and explain limitations in ordinary language.

Evaluate mistaken predictions, changing preferences, and cold starts. A design should preserve exploration and recovery instead of treating model confidence as proof that the person wants the suggested action.

Source: [Apple HIG — Machine learning](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/machine-learning). Checked 2026-09-08.
