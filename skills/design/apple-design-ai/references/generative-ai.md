# Generative AI

- Choose a concrete user benefit and explain the feature’s capabilities and limitations without overstating reliability.
- Keep people able to guide, edit, retry, compare, or undo results, and provide a useful experience when AI is unavailable or declined.
- Explain personal-data handling and obtain appropriate permission for consequential actions.
- Provide meaningful generation progress and helpful recovery from blocked or unsuitable output.
- Evaluate harmful, misleading, or unsupported results and offer useful feedback mechanisms.

Distinguish generated content from verified information and completed action. Validate real outputs and correction paths; a friendly assistant voice does not establish accuracy.

Source: [Apple HIG — Generative AI](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/generative-ai). Checked 2026-09-08.
