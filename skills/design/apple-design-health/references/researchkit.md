# ResearchKit

- Preserve the onboarding order: introduce the study, establish eligibility, obtain informed consent, then request device or data access.
- Break complex consent information into understandable sections and assess understanding when appropriate.
- Make surveys and active tasks clear enough to complete reliably.
- Offer useful personal-data management and a dashboard that explains participation progress.

Do not collapse study consent into a system permission dialog. Verify the study’s approved protocol and current framework requirements with the responsible team; interface clarity alone does not establish valid research consent.

Source: [Apple HIG — ResearchKit](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/researchkit). Checked 2026-09-08.
