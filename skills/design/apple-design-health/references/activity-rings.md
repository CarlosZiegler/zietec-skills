# Activity rings

- Use Activity rings only for an individual’s established Move, Exercise, and Stand information.
- Preserve their native appearance, meanings, colors, and margins.
- Match associated labels to the corresponding metric where color is used.
- Keep unrelated ring-shaped metrics visually distinct.
- Avoid decorative, branding, or duplicate-notification uses.

Check the supported platform presentation, including differences between iPhone configurations and Watch. Do not borrow the recognizable ring treatment for revenue, hydration, team scores, or other unrelated measures.

Source: [Apple HIG — Activity rings](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/activity-rings). Checked 2026-09-08.
