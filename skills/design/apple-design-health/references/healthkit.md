# HealthKit

- Request health information only for an appropriate health or fitness purpose and only when needed.
- Explain the requested data in the system permission experience and use system privacy settings to manage sharing.
- Provide a coherent account of how health information is handled.
- Use Activity rings only for their established individual activity metrics.
- Follow approved Apple Health naming and artwork rules; avoid exposing framework terminology in ordinary user instructions.

Separate interface design from interpreting medical data. Verify current platform and health-data requirements before implementation or any claim about appropriate collection.

Source: [Apple HIG — HealthKit](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/healthkit). Checked 2026-09-08.
