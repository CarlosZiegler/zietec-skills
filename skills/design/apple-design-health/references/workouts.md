# Workouts

- Prioritize relevant metrics and easy-to-use controls during an active workout.
- Visually distinguish the active session and keep text readable while moving.
- Explain what is recorded when sensor data is unavailable.
- Provide a useful completion summary and avoid retaining extremely brief accidental sessions as meaningful workouts.
- Use Activity rings only for their intended metrics.

Review start, pause, resume, end, accidental activation, and missing-data states on the intended device. Do not let unrelated messages or secondary settings distract from the physical activity.

Source: [Apple HIG — Workouts](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/workouts). Checked 2026-09-08.
