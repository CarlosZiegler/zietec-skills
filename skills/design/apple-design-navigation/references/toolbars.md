# Toolbars

- Select frequently needed commands and navigation deliberately. Move secondary actions into an appropriate menu instead of overcrowding the bar.
- Group controls by role and relationship. Keep navigation and critical commit or close actions in clear, familiar groups.
- Use standard Back and Close behavior and meaningful content or window titles.
- Prefer system symbols and toolbar components; distinguish a key action through appropriate prominence.
- Support customization on iPad or Mac when it benefits repeated work.

Verify current platform placement and grouping rules. Reduce unnecessary backgrounds and tinting that compete with the system material. If toolbars hide for immersive content, preserve an obvious way to reveal essential controls.

Source: [Apple HIG — Toolbars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/toolbars). Checked 2026-09-08.
