# Split views

- Use adjacent panes to expose related levels of hierarchy or useful supplementary content.
- Keep the current selection visibly identified in panes that lead to the detail view.
- Define how panes collapse and reappear at compact, narrow, and intermediate widths, preserving the person’s context.
- Set sensible pane limits and offer multiple ways to reveal hidden panes when needed.
- Support drag and drop between panes when the task benefits.

Check each platform’s expected structure. On visionOS, related supplementary information often belongs beside content instead of in a new window. A split view should reveal relationships, not merely divide the canvas into arbitrary columns.

Source: [Apple HIG — Split views](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/split-views). Checked 2026-09-08.
