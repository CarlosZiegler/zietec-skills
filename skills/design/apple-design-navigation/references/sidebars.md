# Sidebars

- Use a sidebar when the available space and hierarchy benefit from persistent navigation. Consider a tab-based structure first on iOS and iPadOS.
- Keep labels concise, group related destinations, and generally limit visible hierarchy to two levels.
- Offer hiding or customization when useful, with a clear way to restore navigation.
- Adapt visibility as windows narrow without losing the selected destination.
- Avoid putting critical Mac actions at the bottom, where a moved window may hide them.

Read current appearance guidance for content extending beneath the sidebar. Choose sidebar styling according to its actual container and platform rather than copying a translucent panel into every layout.

Source: [Apple HIG — Sidebars](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sidebars). Checked 2026-09-08.
