# iMessage apps and stickers

- Focus an iMessage app on one primary experience within the conversation.
- Keep essential functions available in compact presentation and reserve text editing for expanded presentation where appropriate.
- Surface relevant content from the main app when useful.
- Make stickers expressive, inclusive, and useful in varied conversations, with localized alternative descriptions.

Check current icon and sticker specifications before export. Design the composition and selection experience without sending a message as part of an unapproved test.

Source: [Apple HIG — iMessage apps and stickers](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/imessage-apps-and-stickers). Checked 2026-09-08.
