# NFC

- Provide clear, approachable instructions for the scan and use the system scanning experience.
- Avoid implying that the phone must physically touch the object.
- Support appropriate background and in-app reading paths where available.

Verify supported devices and tag behavior for the intended task. Design cancellation, an unreadable tag, and successful handoff so the person understands the result without needing to know the radio technology.

Source: [Apple HIG — NFC](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/nfc). Checked 2026-09-08.
