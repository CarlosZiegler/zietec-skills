# Maps

- Keep maps interactive where useful and choose emphasis suited to the task.
- Make selected places clear, cluster overlapping points, and maintain sufficient contrast for custom controls.
- Preserve required Apple attribution and legal access.
- Avoid duplicate information in place cards and keep the relevant location visible.
- For indoor maps, adjust detail by zoom, expose floors, and retain useful surrounding context.

Review location-denied, dense, sparse, and resized presentations. A map should help people understand place and context rather than serve as an unexplained decorative background.

Source: [Apple HIG — Maps](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/maps). Checked 2026-09-08.
