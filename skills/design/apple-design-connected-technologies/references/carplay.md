# CarPlay

- Design for quick, low-attention interaction while driving, with clear primary content and restrained complexity.
- Keep essential operation and error recovery in CarPlay rather than requiring an unlocked iPhone.
- Preserve expected audio behavior and let people choose playback.
- Maintain legibility across light and dark conditions and test in the vehicle environment.
- Use appropriate artwork and clear interaction styling.

Verify current permitted categories, templates, entitlements, and hardware behavior. A normal iPhone screen cannot simply be projected into CarPlay and considered suitable for the driving context.

Source: [Apple HIG — CarPlay](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/carplay). Checked 2026-09-08.
