# Game Center

- Place social access in appropriate menu contexts without overlapping controls or distracting from play.
- Use current Game Center artwork and terminology.
- Make achievement states and progress understandable with suitable descriptions and assets.
- Choose leaderboards and challenges that reflect meaningful, playable goals.
- Handle overlays and multiplayer activities without losing the game’s state or leaving play running inappropriately.

Verify current support for achievements, challenges, activities, and platform-specific access. Do not assume an older dashboard-only integration represents every current Game Center experience.

Source: [Apple HIG — Game Center](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/game-center). Checked 2026-09-08.
